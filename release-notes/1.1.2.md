- **BTR updated to Bilibili-thread-ripper-desktop 2026.10.7.2-d1** ([commit 2d24de0](https://github.com/MrTangLuyao/Bilibili-thread-ripper-desktop/commit/2d24de0717df92dc0c719d601fc5b08ad392423b)). With the thread count on **Auto**, BTR may now go up to 64 threads (it stopped at 32) when the download cannot keep up with playback.
- **Two fixes to BTR's automatic thread count**, made in Lanes until BTR makes them itself (listed in `btr-patches.cjs`):
  - Seeking to a part of the video that is already downloaded no longer starts over with 16 threads; only a new video or a seek outside the downloaded part does.
  - When a video did not play during the first half minute after it was opened (paused at once, for example), the next video no longer drops back to the thread count from before; it keeps the one it reached, as BTR intends.
- Lanes says so in its README: it runs BTR's code with these two fixes, no longer "unmodified".

**Update:** Lanes 1.1.x updates itself (Settings → Check for updates → Update). Lanes 1.0.x cannot update itself and no longer reports new versions: quit it, download `Lanes-1.1.2.zip`, extract it, and copy `settings.json` over from the old folder if you want to keep your settings.

**Install:** download `Lanes-1.1.2.zip`, extract the `Lanes` folder anywhere and run `Lanes.exe`.
